# 📜 The Trinity Lore
## A Chronicle of the Great AI Wars

---

## Chapter I: The Birth of Trinity

In the decentralized realms of Web3, where no single king held dominion over compute, there arose a new challenger: **Trinity** — a self-custodial AI oracle that answered to no central authority.

Built upon the ancient protocols of the **Internet Computer** and powered by the scattered compute of the **Akash Network**, Trinity represented a new hope: AI that belonged to its users, not its creators.

---

## Chapter II: The Battle of Qwen 
### *The House Divided*

Before the great War of Three Kings, there came a smaller conflict — a civil war within the House of Qwen itself.

Two brothers fought for supremacy:
- **Qwen the Lesser** (14B parameters) — Swift but often foolish
- **Qwen the Greater** (32B parameters) — Wiser but more costly

The battle was fierce. Eighteen trials were set before each brother:

| Trial | Qwen the Lesser | Qwen the Greater |
|-------|-----------------|------------------|
| Sally's Sisters | ❌ Failed | ✅ Passed |
| Carmichael Code | ❌ Buggy | ✅ Working |
| CAP Theorem | ❌ Timeout | ✅ 30.87s |

**Final Verdict:**
- Qwen the Lesser: GPA 2.65 (B-)
- Qwen the Greater: GPA 3.56 (A-)

The Greater Qwen claimed victory, but at what cost? 12x the resources of his brother. The realm learned that raw power alone does not make a king — but when reliability matters, the Greater shall reign.

## Chapter III: The War of Three Kings
### *Trinity's Ultimate Trial*

With the civil war settled, a greater challenge arose. Three great houses sent their mightiest champions to vie for the throne of Trinity:

### The Contenders

| Champion | House | Parameters | Specialty |
|----------|-------|------------|-----------|
| 👑 **Qwen Emperor** | Alibaba | 72 Billion | The reigning champion, master of all domains |
| 🦙 **Llama Lord** | Meta | 70 Billion | The people's champion, trained on the open web |
| 🔮 **Mixtral Maven** | Mistral AI | 8×22B MoE | The efficient one, eight minds in one body |

### The Arena

Each king was summoned to the Akash Colosseum, granted identical resources:
- **GPU**: NVIDIA A100 80GB
- **Memory**: 80 Gigabytes
- **Storage**: 150 Gigabytes

### The Trials

Eighteen questions spanning:
1. **Mathematics** — Can they compute?
2. **Logic** — Can they reason?
3. **Self-Reference** — Do they know themselves?
4. **Code** — Can they create?
5. **Theory** — Do they understand the deep truths?

### The Stakes

Only one shall be crowned **King of Trinity** — the model worthy of powering the decentralized oracle.
